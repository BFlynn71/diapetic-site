FAQ & support

Questions and answers

Quick answers to the most common questions. For step-by-step help, see How to use.

Using the app

I forgot to log a shot. Can I add it later?

Yes. Go to that day on the Tracker (swipe back or use the calendar button), tap Give on the shot, and choose A different time to enter when it was actually given.

What does "Not logged" mean?

The shot's usual time has passed and nothing has been recorded yet. It doesn't mean the shot was missed, only that DiaPetic doesn't know. If reminders are on, you'll get a nudge.

If you're not sure whether it was given, don't give another. Call your veterinarian.

Does DiaPetic tell me how much insulin to give?

No, and it never will. Insulin doses must come from your veterinarian. DiaPetic records the dose your vet prescribed and what was actually given, so your vet has accurate information to make those decisions.

Can I track a cat? More than one pet?

Yes to both. DiaPetic works for dogs and cats. Your first pet is free; adding more pets is part of DiaPetic Pro.

Can two people use it for the same pet?

Not yet. Today each iPhone keeps its own records. Sharing between family members is planned for a future update.

Why didn't my reminder go off?

Check iPhone Settings › Notifications › DiaPetic and make sure notifications are allowed, then check that a Focus mode (like Sleep or Do Not Disturb) isn't silencing them. In DiaPetic, open your pet's tab › Reminders to confirm the reminder is on.

Can I talk instead of typing?

Yes. In the journal and the suggestion box, tap the large microphone and speak. The first time, iPhone asks permission to use the microphone and speech recognition.

Your data and privacy

Where is my information stored?

Only on your iPhone. DiaPetic has no account, no ads, no analytics and no servers. Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to share it, such as sending a report or saving a backup.

Can my veterinarian see my data?

Only what you send them, like a PDF report or an email from the Vet tab. There's no portal or automatic sharing.

How do I move to a new iPhone or restore a backup?

On your old iPhone, go to your pet's tab › Backup and restore › Back up now and save the file to iCloud Drive. On the new iPhone, install DiaPetic, open Backup and restore, choose Restore from backup and pick that file.

If I delete the app, is my data gone?

Yes, unless you have a backup. Deleting an app removes its data from the iPhone. Make a backup first.

Pro and purchases

What does DiaPetic Pro include? Is it a subscription?

Pro is a one-time purchase of $9.99, not a subscription. It adds PDF and CSV reports, 90- and 180-day trends, more than one pet, your own reminders, and dog or kitty app icons with seasonal looks. It's shared with your Family Sharing group.

I bought Pro on another iPhone. How do I get it back?

Open the Pro screen in your pet's tab and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

What are tips?

Optional thank-yous in Support DiaPetic that help cover the cost of keeping the app on the App Store. Tips don't unlock anything. Everything that keeps your pet safe is always free.

How do I change the app icon?

With Pro, go to your pet's tab › App settings › App icon. Pick Obi the dog or the kitty, and a style: automatic seasons, always regular, or one theme all year.

Still need help?

Email us and include your iPhone model and what you were doing. Suggestions are very welcome too. You can also send one from the app: your pet's tab › App settings › Suggestion box.
