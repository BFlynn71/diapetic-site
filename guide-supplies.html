Insulin, syringes and food

DiaPetic keeps track of supplies from the shots and meals you log, and warns you before anything runs out.

On this page
  1. Insulin bottles
  2. Prescription details
  3. Syringes
  4. Food on hand

Insulin bottles

Open your pet's tab › Insulin bottles, or tap Manage in Supplies on the Tracker.

  • In use: turn on Bottle opened, set the date you opened it, the units in the bottle, and the expiration date on the label. Set how many days an opened bottle is good for; check the insulin's label or ask your vet. A 10 mL bottle holds 400 units of U-40 insulin or 1,000 units of U-100.
  • In the fridge: tap Got a new bottle to add unopened bottles, with the expiration date and lot number. Bottles are opened in order of expiration date, earliest first, and you're warned if one will expire before its turn.
  • Starting the next bottle: tap Open new bottle. The old bottle moves to history and the next spare becomes the one in use. Tapped it by mistake? Undo is there for the rest of the day.

Prescription details

In the same screen, record the date filled, refills left, the Rx number (tap to copy it when you call) and the pharmacy. When no refills are left, you'll get a reminder to ask your vet for a new prescription before the insulin runs out.

Syringes

Open your pet's tab › Syringes. Set when you started the box in use and how many it holds; DiaPetic counts one syringe per shot. Tap Got new syringes to add unopened boxes.

When you start a new box, tap Start new box. If there are syringes left in the old box, choose whether you're keeping the leftovers (they're added to the new count) or threw them away, so the count stays right even when boxes overlap.

Food on hand

Track bags and cans in each food's settings (see Food on hand). The Supplies section shows when each food runs out.
