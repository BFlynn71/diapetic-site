Insulin, syringes and food

DiaPetic tracks your supplies based on the shots and meals you record, and alerts you before anything runs out.

On this page

Insulin on hand

The top of your pet's tab › Insulin Supplies › Insulin bottles shows how many days are left in the bottle in use and a timeline of the dates that matter: today, when to open the next bottle, the date to act on and when the insulin on hand runs out. The date to act on is highlighted in orange:

  • Order a refill by when refills remain on the prescription.
  • Refill due by when Auto refill is on, so you can check that the pharmacy's refill has arrived.
  • Ask your vet by when no refills remain. This date comes a week earlier, to allow time for a new prescription.

The dates use the dose currently entered under Insulin and shot times. When your veterinarian changes the dose and you enter it, the dates update right away. They match the supply reminders.

Insulin bottles

Open your pet's tab › Insulin Supplies › Insulin bottles, or tap Manage in the Insulin Supplies section of the Tracker. Below the timeline, Bottle in use, Unopened bottles and Prescription each open their own page.

Bottle in use. Turn on Bottle opened and enter the date it was opened, the number of units in the bottle and the expiration date on the label. Then set how many days an opened bottle remains usable, as stated on the insulin's label or by your veterinarian. For reference, a 10 mL bottle contains 400 units of U-40 insulin or 1,000 units of U-100 insulin.

Spare bottles. Tap Got a new bottle to record unopened bottles with their expiration dates and lot numbers. DiaPetic suggests opening them in order of expiration and warns you if a bottle is likely to expire before it is needed.

Starting a new bottle. Tap Open new bottle. The current bottle moves to the history and the next spare becomes the bottle in use. If you tap it by mistake, Undo is available for the rest of the day.

Prescription details

On the same screen, you can record the date the prescription was filled, the number of refills left, the prescription number and the pharmacy. The prescription number can be copied when you call to reorder. Turn on Auto refill if the pharmacy sends refills on its own; the supply reminder then asks you to check that the refill has arrived instead of asking you to order. When no refills remain, DiaPetic reminds you to request a new prescription from your veterinarian before the insulin runs out.

Syringes

The Syringes page works like Insulin bottles. The top shows how many days are left in the box in use and a timeline with today, when to start the next box, the date to order more (in orange) and when the syringes on hand run out. Below it, Box in use, Unopened boxes and Past boxes each open their own page.

Open your pet's tab › Insulin Supplies › Syringes and enter the date you started the current box and how many syringes it contains. DiaPetic counts one syringe for each shot. Record unopened boxes with Got new syringes.

Check the box for U-40 or U-100 and set it under Marked on the box. If the syringes do not match your pet's insulin, DiaPetic shows a warning on the Tracker and in your pet's tab. A syringe of the wrong strength gives the wrong dose: U-100 syringes with U-40 insulin give about 40% of the prescribed dose, and U-40 syringes with U-100 insulin give about 2.5 times the prescribed dose. Do not try to convert the dose yourself. Call your veterinarian before the next shot.

When you open a new box, tap Start new box. If syringes remain in the previous box, indicate whether you are keeping them, in which case they are added to the new count, or have discarded them. This keeps the count accurate when boxes overlap.

Food on hand

Food supplies are tracked in each food's settings, as described in Food on hand. The Insulin Supplies section on the Tracker also shows when each food is expected to run out.
