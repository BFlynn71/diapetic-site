Insulin, syringes and food

DiaPetic tracks your supplies based on the shots and meals you record, and alerts you before anything runs out.

Insulin on hand

The top of your pet's tab › Insulin Supplies › Insulin bottles shows how many days are left in the bottle in use and a timeline of the dates that matter: today, when to open the next bottle, the date to act on and when the insulin on hand runs out. The date to act on is highlighted in orange:

  • Order a refill by when refills remain on the prescription.
  • Refill due by when Auto refill is on, so you can check that the pharmacy's refill has arrived.
  • Ask your vet by when no refills remain. This date comes a week earlier, to allow time for a new prescription.

The dates use the dose currently entered under Insulin and shot times. When your veterinarian changes the dose and you enter it, the dates update right away. They match the supply reminders.

When Auto refill deliveries are set up, the timeline also shows the next Delivery. If the insulin on hand would run out before that delivery, DiaPetic shows a warning so you can call the pharmacy or your veterinarian in time.

Insulin bottles

Your pet's tab › Insulin Supplies shows the same insulin and syringe cards as the Insulin Supplies section of the Tracker, with the same buttons. Tap Manage on either one to open the Insulin bottles page. Below the timeline, Opened on shows when the bottle in use was opened, and Bottle in use, Unopened bottles and Prescription each open their own page.

Bottle in use. Turn on Bottle opened and enter the date it was opened, the number of units in the bottle and the expiration date on the label. Then set how many days an opened bottle remains usable, as stated on the insulin's label or by your veterinarian. For reference, a 10 mL bottle contains 400 units of U-40 insulin or 1,000 units of U-100 insulin.

Spare bottles. Tap Got a new bottle to record unopened bottles with their expiration dates and lot numbers. DiaPetic suggests opening them in order of expiration and warns you if a bottle is likely to expire before it is needed.

Starting a new bottle. Tap Open new bottle and choose when you opened it: Just now, or Earlier with the date and time. Shots recorded from that moment are counted against the new bottle. The current bottle moves to the history and the next spare becomes the bottle in use. If you tap it by mistake, Undo is available for the rest of the day.

Changing the open date. If you opened a bottle before recording it, tap Opened … Edit under the bottle on the Tracker, or Opened on on the Insulin bottles page, and choose the day. You can add the time as well; without a time, DiaPetic counts from the start of that day. The units left, the day of use, the use-by date and the supply dates are recalculated from every shot recorded since then, and the previous bottle's finish date moves to match. Only shots recorded in DiaPetic are counted.

Prescription details

On the same screen, you can record the date the prescription was filled, the number of refills left, the prescription number and the pharmacy. The prescription number can be copied when you call to reorder. Turn on Auto refill if the pharmacy sends refills on its own; the supply reminder then asks you to check that the refill has arrived instead of asking you to order. When no refills remain, DiaPetic reminds you to request a new prescription from your veterinarian before the insulin runs out.

Deliveries. With Auto refill on, enter the Next delivery date, How often it comes (every week, every 2 or 3 weeks, every 30, 60 or 90 days, or a custom number of days up to 90) and how many bottles come in Each delivery. On delivery day, DiaPetic sends a reminder in the morning that the insulin is expected, and the Supplies cards show Delivery expected today. When it arrives, tap It arrived: the bottles are added to the fridge, one refill is counted off the prescription and the next delivery date moves forward. If it has not been recorded by 8 PM, or by the next morning, DiaPetic reminds you again. Nothing is added until you tap It arrived.

Syringes

The Syringes page works like Insulin bottles. The top shows how many days are left in the box in use and a timeline with today, when to start the next box, the date to order more (in orange) and when the syringes on hand run out. Below it, Box in use, Unopened boxes and Past boxes each open their own page.

Tap Manage on the syringe card, on the Tracker or in your pet's tab › Insulin Supplies, and enter the date you started the current box and how many syringes it contains. DiaPetic counts one syringe for each shot. To change the start date later, tap Started … Edit on the card or Started on on the Syringes page. Record unopened boxes with Got new syringes.

If the pharmacy sends syringes on its own, open Prescription on the Syringes page, turn on Auto refill and enter the next delivery date, how often it comes and how many boxes come in each delivery. Deliveries then work the same way as for insulin, with an It arrived button and reminders on delivery day.

Check the box for U-40 or U-100 and set it under Marked on the box. If the syringes do not match your pet's insulin, DiaPetic shows a warning on the Tracker and in your pet's tab. A syringe of the wrong strength gives the wrong dose: U-100 syringes with U-40 insulin give about 40% of the prescribed dose, and U-40 syringes with U-100 insulin give about 2.5 times the prescribed dose. Do not try to convert the dose yourself. Call your veterinarian before the next shot.

When you open a new box, tap Start new box and choose Just now or Earlier. If syringes remain in the previous box, indicate whether you are still using them, in which case they are added to the new count, or have thrown them away. This keeps the count accurate when boxes overlap.

Food on hand

Food supplies are tracked in each food's settings, as described in Food on hand. The Insulin Supplies section on the Tracker also shows when each food is expected to run out.
