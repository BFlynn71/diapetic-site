Backups and a new iPhone

Everything you enter is stored only on your iPhone. A backup is the only way to get it back if the phone is lost or the app is deleted.

On this page
  1. Back up now
  2. Automatic weekly copy
  3. Restoring and moving to a new iPhone

Back up now

Open your pet's tab › Backup and restore and tap Back up now. Save the file to iCloud Drive so it's safe even if your iPhone isn't. The backup has every pet, all logs, visits, bottles, syringe boxes, journal entries, reminders and settings.

Automatic weekly copy

Once a week DiaPetic saves a copy by itself and keeps the last four. If those copies are only on the iPhone, they'd be lost with it, so use Back up now from time to time too.

Restoring and moving to a new iPhone

  1. On the new iPhone, install DiaPetic and open Backup and restore.
  2. Tap Restore from backup… and pick your backup file.
  3. Check what's in it (pets and days of logs), then tap Restore.

Restoring replaces what's currently in DiaPetic; a copy of the current data is saved first, just in case. If you have Pro, open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchase.
