Backups and a new iPhone

Everything you enter in DiaPetic is stored only on your iPhone. A backup is the only way to recover your records if your phone is lost or the app is deleted.

On this page
  1. Making a backup
  2. Automatic weekly copies
  3. Restoring or moving to a new iPhone

Making a backup

Go to your pet's tab › Backup and restore and tap Back up now. Save the file to iCloud Drive so it remains safe even if something happens to your iPhone. A backup includes every pet and all records, visits, insulin bottles, syringe boxes, journal entries, reminders and settings.

Automatic weekly copies

DiaPetic also saves a copy automatically once a week and keeps the four most recent. Because these copies may be stored only on the iPhone, it is a good idea to use Back up now regularly as well.

Restoring or moving to a new iPhone

  1. Install DiaPetic on the new iPhone and open Backup and restore.
  2. Tap Restore from backup… and select your backup file.
  3. Review the contents of the backup, including the pets and number of days recorded, and tap Restore.

Restoring replaces the data currently in DiaPetic, and a copy of that data is saved first as a precaution. If you purchased DiaPetic Pro, open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchase.
