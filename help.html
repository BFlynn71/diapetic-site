How to use

The DiaPetic guide

This guide explains each part of DiaPetic in detail. If you are new to the app, begin with Getting started and then read The Daily Tracker. You can open the guide from within the app at any time under your pet’s tab › Help and support. The illustrations show a sample pet named Biscuit.

1Getting startedInstalling DiaPetic and setting up your pet.2The Daily TrackerRecording shots, meals, the check-in, weight, glucose and journal entries.3Food and nutritionMeal plans, calories, the label scanner, food on hand and diet changes.4Medications and supplementsAdding medications and supplements, schedules and doses.5Glucose and lab resultsHome glucose readings, curves, time in range and laboratory results.6RemindersReminders for shots, meals, medications, supplies, checkups and more.7Insulin, syringes and foodInsulin bottles, prescriptions, syringes and food supplies.8Your veterinarianClinic contacts, appointments, visit notes, questions and email.9Reports and sharingProgress and glucose reports, PDF and CSV files, and the pet information sheet.10When something seems wrongEmergency help, guided questions, quick guides and the clinic summary.11Your pet's tab and settingsPet details, multiple pets, app settings and the app icon.12Backups and a new iPhoneBacking up, automatic copies, restoring and moving to a new iPhone.

Please note: DiaPetic keeps records and sends reminders. It does not recommend insulin doses or changes to treatment. Always follow your veterinarian’s instructions.

If you cannot find what you need, see the FAQ or email support@diapetic.com.
