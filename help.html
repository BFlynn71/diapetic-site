How to use

The DiaPetic guide

Everything DiaPetic does, one area at a time. New? Start with Getting started, then read The Daily Tracker. You can open this guide from the app anytime: your pet’s tab › App settings › Help & how-to. The pictures show a sample pet, Biscuit.

1Getting startedInstall, add your pet and set things up in about 10 minutes.2The Daily TrackerLogging shots, meals, the check-in, weight, glucose and the journal.3Food and nutritionMeal plans, calories, the label scanner, food on hand and diet changes.4Medications and supplementsOther medicines, supplements, schedules and doses.5Glucose and lab resultsHome readings, curves, time in range and lab work.6RemindersShots, missed-shot nudges, meals, medications, supplies, checkups and your own.7Insulin, syringes and foodBottles in use and in the fridge, prescriptions, syringe boxes and food on hand.8Your veterinarianContacts, appointments, visit notes, questions and email.9Reports and sharingProgress, glucose, PDF and CSV reports, and the pet info sheet.10When something seems wrongThe emergency button, triage questions, guides and clinic summary.11Your pet's tab and settingsPet details, weight, more than one pet, app settings and the app icon.12Backups and a new iPhoneBack up, automatic copies, restore and moving phones.

Remember: DiaPetic keeps records and reminds you. It never recommends insulin doses or changes to treatment. Always follow your veterinarian’s instructions.
