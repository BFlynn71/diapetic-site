How to use

The DiaPetic guide

This guide explains each part of DiaPetic in detail. If you are new to the app, begin with Getting started and then read The Daily Tracker. You can open the guide from within the app at any time under your pet’s tab › Help and support. The illustrations show a sample pet named Biscuit.

Guides for owners

Longer guides on keeping records and getting ready for vet visits.

1How to track a dog or cat's glucose curve at homeYour vet asked for a glucose curve. Learn what to record, how to keep each reading in order, and how to share a clear curve with your dog or cat's vet.2Managing twice-daily pet insulin shots and meal schedulesHow to keep twice-daily insulin shots and meals on time for a diabetic dog or cat, share care at home, and help prevent missed or double doses.3What to bring to a diabetic pet vet appointmentA checklist for diabetic dog and cat vet visits: the records, supplies and questions to bring, and how to share a 30- to 180-day summary with your vet.4How to track pet insulin bottles, syringes and prescription food suppliesKeep track of your diabetic pet's insulin bottles, syringes and prescription food: open dates, matching syringes, refills and spares, so nothing runs out.

Please note: DiaPetic keeps records and sends reminders. It does not recommend insulin doses or changes to treatment. Always follow your veterinarian’s instructions.
