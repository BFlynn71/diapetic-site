Your pet's tab and settings

The last tab is named after your pet. It holds everything you set up, plus the app's settings.

On this page
  1. Pet details
  2. More than one pet
  3. App settings

Pet details

Change your pet's name, species, breed, color, sex and microchip number. The Weight section shows the weight trend and body condition score, plus the starting and target weight from your vet. Below that are the meal plan, insulin and shot times, other medications, reminders, supplies and home glucose settings.

More than one pet

Tap the pet menu at the top left of any tab to switch pets or Add a pet. Your first pet is free; more pets are part of DiaPetic Pro. Each pet has its own log, reminders, reports and supplies.

App settings

  • Appearance: match iPhone, light or dark.
  • Glucose units (mg/dL or mmol/L) and weight units (lb or kg).
  • App icon (Pro): Obi the dog or Kitty, with automatic seasonal looks (fall, Halloween, Thanksgiving, holiday season and Christmas), always regular, or one theme all year.
  • Backup and restore: see Backups and a new iPhone.
  • Help & how-to (this guide), the Suggestion box, Rate DiaPetic, and Support DiaPetic (optional tips).
  • About DiaPetic: privacy, the website, FAQ and the privacy policy.

Deleting a pet

At the bottom of the tab, Delete … and all records permanently removes every shot, reading and check-in for that pet. Make a backup first if you might want them later.

Suggestions and support

The Suggestion box sends a message from your own email: choose a suggestion, something that isn't working, a question or something you love, then talk or type. You can include app and iPhone details to help track down problems; your pet's health records are never included. Or email support@diapetic.com.
