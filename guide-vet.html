Your veterinarian

The Vet tab keeps everything about your pet's veterinary care in one place.

Contacts and finding clinics

In Vet tab › Contacts › Edit or find clinics, enter your vet's clinic, the doctor's name, phone, email and address, plus an emergency clinic for after-hours problems such as very low blood sugar. Tap Find veterinarians near me or Find emergency clinics near me to search with your location or a city or ZIP code, then choose Use as my veterinarian or Use as my emergency clinic. Your location is only used for the search.

Appointments

  1. Tap Schedule an appointment.
  2. Choose the type: checkup, glucose curve, blood work or fructosamine, recheck, dental, vaccines, emergency visit, or other.
  3. Set when it starts and ends (drop-off and pick-up for a day stay), where it is, and any instructions from the clinic, such as "drop off by 8 AM".
  4. List what to bring. It's shown in the reminders and on the Tracker the day before.
  5. Choose reminders, and tap Add to Calendar to put it on your iPhone calendar. If you change or cancel the appointment in DiaPetic, the calendar event is updated or removed too.
Ask the clinic whether to feed and give insulin as usual that morning, and write their answer in the instructions.

After the visit

After an appointment, DiaPetic asks how it went. Tap Add visit notes to record what the veterinarian said and the next steps (dose changes, rechecks, diet), add a lab result, or schedule a follow-up. If it didn't happen, tap It didn't happen. If the insulin dose changed, update it in your pet's tab so reports show the change.

Questions for your vet

Jot questions down as they come up under Questions for your veterinarian. Open questions are printed at the top of the PDF report and can be included when you email your vet. Tap the circle when one is answered, and add the answer if you like.

Emailing your vet

Tap Ask your vet to write an email: choose questions to include, add a message, and optionally include a summary of the last 7 days, 2 weeks or 30 days. With Pro you can attach the PDF report. Tap Preview to see it, then send from your own email, or choose Share another way for Gmail or Messages.
