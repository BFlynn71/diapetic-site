Medications and supplements

Add everything your pet takes besides insulin in your pet's tab › Other medications and supplements.

On this page
  1. Adding a medication
  2. On the Tracker
  3. Stopping a medication
  4. Reminders

Adding a medication

  1. Tap Add medication or supplement and choose which it is.
  2. Enter the name (for example Vetoryl), the dose as prescribed (for example 60 mg capsule) and, if you like, what it's for.
  3. Choose the schedule: with the morning routine, with the evening routine, morning and evening, once a day, or as needed.
  4. If a dose has calories (fish oil, chews, pastes), enter them so the day's calories add up.
  5. Set when it started and add notes such as "give with food".

On the Tracker

Scheduled medications appear in the morning or evening card to check off; once-a-day supplements show as a daily checkbox; as-needed medications have a Give button. Reports show the percent of scheduled doses checked off.

Stopping a medication

When your pet stops a medication, turn on Stopped and set the date rather than deleting it. That keeps its history in reports. Delete only something added by mistake.

Reminders

Morning and evening medications are listed in the shot reminder. Once-a-day medications can have their own reminder, and you can be reminded before a course ends. Set these in your pet's tab › Reminders › Medications and supplements.
