Medications and supplements

Record everything your pet takes in addition to insulin in your pet's tab › Supplements and your pet's tab › Medications.

Adding a medication or supplement

  1. Open Supplements or Medications and tap Add supplement or Add medication.
  2. Enter the name, the dose as prescribed (for example, "60 mg capsule") and, if you wish, its purpose.
  3. For a medication, choose a schedule: with the morning routine, with the evening routine, morning and evening, once a day, or as needed. For a supplement, choose when it is given: with the morning meal, with the evening meal, with both meals, or once a day and not with a meal.
  4. If a dose contains calories, as fish oil and chews often do, enter them so they are included in the day's total.
  5. Enter the start date and any notes, such as "give with food."

On the Tracker

Scheduled medications appear in the morning or evening card, ready to check off. Supplements given with a meal appear under that meal. Other supplements are a daily check-off in the Supplements tile, and medications given as needed have a Give button. Reports show the percentage of scheduled doses that were recorded.

Giving a medication at its own times

When a medication does not fit the morning and evening routine, such as an ointment or ear drops, choose My own schedule under When. This is part of DiaPetic Gold, a one-time purchase with no subscription.

  • Set one to four times a day, or use Space evenly from the first time to fill them in every 12, 8 or 6 hours.
  • Choose whether it repeats every day, every other day or once a week. Repeats count from the start date.
  • Record how it is given and, if useful, where, such as the left ear.
  • Turn on Remind me at each time for a reminder at every dose. Press and hold the reminder and tap Done to check it off without opening the app.

Each time appears as its own row in the Medications section of Tracker, and your reports show doses logged against doses scheduled.

Stopping a medication

When your pet stops taking a medication, turn on Stopped and enter the date rather than deleting it. This keeps its history in your reports. Deleting is best reserved for entries added by mistake.

Medication reminders

Medications on their own schedule can have their own reminders. Morning and evening medications, and supplements given with a meal, are listed in the shot reminder. Once-a-day medications and supplements can have a separate reminder, and DiaPetic can also remind you before a course of medication ends. These options are in your pet's tab › Reminders › Medications and supplements.
