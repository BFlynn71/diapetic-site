Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective October 17, 2026

The short version: DiaPetic doesn't collect your data. What you enter stays on your iPhone. There's no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer has no server that receives your information.

Who we are

DiaPetic is an iPhone app made by Bryan Flynn ("we", "us"). Contact: support@diapetic.com.

Information you enter

Your pet's details, shots, meals, check-ins, glucose readings, journal entries, supplies, appointments and settings are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. We can't see them.

When information leaves your iPhone

Only when you choose:

  • Sharing: reports, the pet info sheet and CSV files go wherever you send them (email, Messages, AirDrop, Files, a printer).
  • Email: emails to your veterinarian and messages from the suggestion box are sent from your own email app, and you see them before they're sent. Messages you send to us are used only to reply and improve the app.
  • Backups: backup files are saved where you choose, such as iCloud Drive or Files. If they're in iCloud, they're stored in your own iCloud account under Apple's terms.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Notifications: to show the reminders you turn on.
  • Calendar: to add your pet's appointments to your calendar and update or remove them when you change them in the app. DiaPetic doesn't read or share your other events.
  • Camera: only to read calorie information from pet food labels. Nothing is photographed or saved.
  • Location: only to find veterinarians and emergency clinics near you, using Apple Maps. It isn't stored.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: only while you dictate a journal entry or suggestion. Recordings aren't saved; only the text is kept. Speech is recognized on your iPhone when it supports that; otherwise Apple processes it under Apple's privacy policy.

You can turn any of these off in iPhone Settings › DiaPetic.

Purchases and ratings

DiaPetic Pro and tips are handled by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details. Apple may share anonymous sales information with us, such as the number of purchases. Rating prompts are shown by Apple.

Children

DiaPetic is meant for adults caring for a pet and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone.

This website

diapetic.com doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking. It's hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Not veterinary advice

DiaPetic is a record-keeping tool. It never recommends insulin doses or changes to treatment and isn't a substitute for veterinary care.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date. If a change affects how your information is handled, we'll also note it in the app's update notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@diapetic.com
