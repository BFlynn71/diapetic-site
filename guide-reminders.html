Reminders

Set everything up in your pet's tab › Reminders. The top of the screen shows what's coming up, so you can check reminders are set the way you want.

Insulin

  • At each shot time: a reminder at the morning and evening shot times.
  • Nudge if a shot isn't logged: a follow-up if nothing has been logged, after 15 minutes to 2 hours (you choose). It helps prevent both a missed dose and a double dose.

Meals

A meal reminder with the shot, or 15, 30 or 45 minutes before it. Fill the bowl lists the planned portions, so whoever feeds your pet knows exactly what to give.

Medications, glucose and logging

  • Morning and evening medications, once-a-day medications and supplements, and a heads-up before a course ends.
  • Glucose checks before each shot, and a reminder to check on your pet after a low reading.
  • A daily log reminder and a weekly weigh-in.

Supplies

Warnings before insulin, syringes or food run low (you choose how many days ahead), and Time to open a new bottle based on how long an opened bottle is good for. Unopened bottles and boxes you've recorded are counted, so you won't be told to order more when spares are waiting.

Checkups

Remind me to book a checkup every 3, 6 or 12 months, counted from your last completed visit, and only when nothing is already scheduled. Reminders for booked appointments are set on each appointment in the Vet tab.

Your own reminders (Pro)

Tap Add a reminder for anything else. Presets include heartworm prevention, flea and tick prevention, refilling the insulin prescription, a fructosamine or glucose curve, vaccines, nail trims, grooming, brushing teeth and cleaning the water bowl, or choose Something else…. Set the first reminder and how often it repeats: once, daily, weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly, every 3 or 6 months, or yearly.

Snoozing and troubleshooting

Each reminder has buttons to snooze for 15 minutes or an hour. Tapping a reminder opens the right pet and screen.

If reminders don't appear, open iPhone Settings › Notifications › DiaPetic and allow notifications, and check that a Focus mode such as Sleep isn't silencing them. If notifications are off, the Reminders screen shows an Open Settings button.
