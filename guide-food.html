Food and nutrition

Consistent meals help keep glucose steady. Set up the meal plan in your pet's tab › Meals and treats.

On this page
  1. Adding a food
  2. Nutrition facts (optional)
  3. Food on hand
  4. Calorie target and portions
  5. Diet changes

Adding a food

  1. Tap Add meal food (or Add treat).
  2. Pick the type: dry kibble, wet or canned, fresh or cooked, raw, topper or mix-in, treat, or other.
  3. Enter the brand and product, the unit (cups, grams, cans and so on), how much is fed, and which meal it's fed at.
  4. Add the calories. Tap Scan the label to point the camera at the calorie statement on the bag or can and tap the number to use it.
Weighing food in grams on a kitchen scale is the most accurate. A level measuring cup can vary by 20% or more.

Nutrition facts (optional)

Enter the guaranteed analysis from the label (protein, fat, fiber, moisture and ash) and DiaPetic shows the values on a dry-matter basis, including estimated carbohydrate. This lets your vet compare foods with different moisture, like kibble and canned. Carbohydrate and fiber matter most for diabetic pets.

Food on hand

Turn on Track how much is left, then enter how it's sold (bags, cans, trays…), the size of each and how many you have. DiaPetic counts down as you log meals, shows when it will run out, and can remind you before it does.

Calorie target and portions

  • Enter the daily calorie target from your veterinarian. If you don't have one, DiaPetic shows what the meal plan adds up to, and you can tap Use as target.
  • Portion estimates set how much "Most" and "Some" count for when you tap them on the Tracker. For exact numbers, log a meal with the amount offered and the amount left over.
  • Treats and "something else" entries count toward the day's calories when you include calories for them.

Diet changes

Changes to the meal plan are recorded automatically. Reports mark each diet change on the charts and compare average glucose, calories and weight before and after, so your vet can see whether a new food made a difference.
