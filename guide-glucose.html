Glucose and lab results

Only check glucose at home if your veterinarian asked you to. Turn it on in your pet's tab › Home glucose monitoring › I check glucose at home.

On this page
  1. Your highlight numbers
  2. Adding a reading
  3. Glucose curves
  4. Lab results

Your highlight numbers

Set Highlight readings below and Highlight readings above to the numbers your veterinarian gives you. They only change how readings are colored, when the low-reading alert shows, and the time-in-range figure. Choose mg/dL or mmol/L in App settings.

Adding a reading

On the Tracker, tap Add reading in Blood glucose. Enter the value, the time, and the source: glucose meter, CGM sensor, or at the vet. Add a note if anything was unusual. A reading below your low number shows a warning with call buttons, and you can turn on a reminder to check on your pet afterward.

Glucose curves

A curve is a series of readings over a day. Open Reports and switch to Glucose to see your readings as a curve, glucose by week, time in range, eating and glucose, and low readings.

Tap Compare curves to overlay up to three days. Your veterinarian looks at the lowest point of the curve and when it happens to judge how well the insulin is working.

Lab results

Record blood work such as fructosamine in Reports › Lab results › Add lab result, or after a vet visit. Enter the test, result, unit and date, plus what the veterinarian said. Lab results are included in reports.
