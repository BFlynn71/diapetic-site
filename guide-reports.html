Reports and sharing

The Reports tab shows how things are going. Choose a period at the top: 7 days, 2 weeks or 30 days (free), or 90 and 180 days (Pro). Switch between Progress and Glucose.

On this page
  1. Progress
  2. PDF report for your vet (Pro)
  3. Pet info sheet (free)

Progress

  • At a glance: food eaten, calories, shots given and weight, compared with the period before.
  • Routine at a glance: typical meal and shot times worked out from your log, and how complete the log is, so your vet knows how much to rely on it.
  • Insulin dose: every change to the prescribed dose, recorded automatically.
  • Symptoms and shot consistency: day-by-day grids, plus shot time against planned time. Shots about 12 hours apart, at the same times each day, help keep glucose steady.
  • Journal: your latest entries, with all of them a tap away.
  • Nutrition: calories per day, appetite, meals and insulin timing, calories and weight, treats, water intake and diet changes.
  • Medications, weight and lab results.

PDF report for your vet (Pro)

Tap Share a PDF report with your veterinarian, choose the period, and tap Create PDF report, then share it by email, Messages or AirDrop, save it to Files, or print it. The report starts with your open questions and the routine at a glance, then progress, glucose and curves, symptoms and shot consistency, nutrition and diet details, episodes and changes (low readings, small meals, missed shots, medications), a day-by-day log, and your journal in full.

CSV file (Pro) exports every entry for Numbers or Excel.

Pet info sheet (free)

In your pet's tab, tap Share beside the name for a one-page sheet with your pet's details, microchip, insulin routine, other medications, meals, vet and emergency contacts, supplies, and low-blood-sugar signs. It's ideal for pet sitters, boarding, family and emergency visits.
