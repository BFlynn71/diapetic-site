Getting started

This chapter walks you through your first ten minutes with DiaPetic. Have your veterinarian's instructions, the insulin bottle or box, and your pet's food bag nearby.

How the app is laid out

DiaPetic has five tabs along the bottom:

  • Tracker: today's page, where you log shots, meals, the check-in and the journal.
  • Reports: how things are going over 7 days to 6 months, plus glucose charts and PDF reports.
  • Vet: appointments, questions for your veterinarian, contacts and email.
  • History: every day you've logged, newest first. Tap a day to see or fix it.
  • Your pet's name: your pet's details, insulin, food, medications, reminders, supplies and app settings.

Two things are on every screen: the pet menu at the top left (switch pets or add one) and the red emergency button at the top right.

First launch

  1. Read the short note on the welcome screen and tap I understand. DiaPetic is a record-keeping tool. It never recommends doses, and it isn't a substitute for your veterinarian.
  2. The pet setup screen opens. Everything on it can be changed later, so don't worry about getting it perfect.

Your pet

  • Name and species: dog, cat or other.
  • Breed: pick from the list, or type your own.
  • Color: choose from common colors for dogs or cats, or pick Other… and describe the color or markings.
  • Sex and whether your pet is spayed or neutered. (Hormone cycles in unspayed females can change how insulin works, so your vet may want to talk about spaying.)
  • Current weight in lb or kg.
  • Microchip # (optional). It appears on the info sheet you can share, which helps if your pet is ever lost.

Insulin

  1. Choose your insulin from the list, and check that the strength matches the bottle: U-40 or U-100. Use syringes that match the insulin's strength.
  2. Enter the units and time for the morning and evening shots, exactly as your vet prescribed. If your pet only gets one shot a day, leave the other one blank.
  3. Leave Remind me at each shot time on, unless you'd rather not have reminders.
DiaPetic records the dose your vet prescribed. It never suggests or changes a dose. When your vet changes the dose, update it in your pet's tab and the change is recorded in your reports automatically.

Meals and treats

  1. Choose a feeding style: set meals (the usual for pets on insulin) or free feeding.
  2. Tap Add meal food for each food your pet eats: the type (dry kibble, wet, fresh, raw, topper), brand, how much, and which meal it's fed at. You can add calories now or later. The Food and nutrition chapter has the details.
  3. Add favorite treats the same way, so they're one tap to log.
  4. If your vet gave you a daily calorie target, enter it.

Your vet and an emergency clinic

Add your veterinarian's clinic and phone, plus an after-hours emergency clinic. These make the call and directions buttons work, including on the emergency screen.

Later, in Vet tab › Contacts › Edit or find clinics, you can add the doctor's name, email and address, or search for clinics near you.

Allow notifications

Tap Save. When iPhone asks whether DiaPetic can send notifications, tap Allow, or reminders can't appear. DiaPetic only asks for other permissions (calendar, camera, location, microphone) when you first use a feature that needs one.

Your first week: a checklist

  • Log every shot and meal on the Tracker for a few days to build the habit.
  • Check that reminders arrive at the right times. Adjust them in your pet's tab › Reminders.
  • Add any other medications or supplements in your pet's tab › Other medications and supplements.
  • Record the insulin bottle you're using and any spares in your pet's tab › Insulin bottles.
  • Add your next vet appointment in the Vet tab.
  • Make your first backup: your pet's tab › Backup and restore › Back up now.
