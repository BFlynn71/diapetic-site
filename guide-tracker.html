The Daily Tracker

The Tracker is where you record each day's care. It always opens on today, and if you return to the app after more than five minutes, or on a new day, it returns to today automatically.

Moving between days

Swipe left or right, or use the ‹ › arrows beside the date, to move one day at a time. To go further back, tap the calendar button beside "Daily Tracker" and choose a date, or use one of the shortcuts for one week, two weeks or one month ago. Tap the date at any time to return to today.

When you change days, the page keeps your place, so you can compare the same section from one day to the next.

To see your pet's card without leaving the Tracker, pull down on the DiaPetic logo at the top, or tap the small bar under it. See Your pet's card.

Summary and alerts

At a glance sits under the date and shows up to five rings for the day on screen. Choose them with Edit: meals, shots, calories, medications, supplements, the check-in, glucose and insulin left. Each ring fills as the day goes on and shows a count in the middle, with a small paw at the end of the colored arc. When a ring is complete, such as both meals logged, the count changes to a paw. A skipped shot fills the Shots ring but does not earn the paw. When the check-in found everything normal, its ring shows a paw. Calories needs a daily calorie target, and glucose needs home glucose checks turned on; if either is missing, DiaPetic offers to set it up. Insulin left always shows the bottle in use today. Each pet keeps its own choice.

The morning and evening routines share one card with two tabs, like folder dividers: Morning on the left and Evening on the right. The routine for the time of day is in front, and the evening routine takes over halfway between your two shot times. The other routine's tab peeks out from behind with its status, such as Later or Done; tap it to bring that routine to the front and check or change anything. The colored band under the tabs shows how many of the routine's items are done. When both routines are finished, the card shows All done for today; tap it to see the details.

If the morning shot still is not logged when the evening routine takes over, a banner at the top asks you to log it or mark it skipped, and the Morning tab shows a warning badge. If the shot was given, log it so it is not given twice. If not, follow your veterinarian's instructions for a late dose.

Below the routines, Today's log shows tiles for check-ins, glucose, medications, treats, walks, the journal and weight. Each tile shows where things stand, and tapping it opens that section. Alerts appear at the top of the page when something needs attention:

  • Eating less than usual appears when recent meals have been smaller than normal.
  • Low reading appears when a glucose reading falls below your low threshold, with buttons to call your veterinarian or emergency clinic.
  • An appointment reminder appears the day before and the day of a visit, along with what to bring.

Recording a meal

Each routine begins with the meal. Tap All, Most, Some or None to record how much your pet ate. To clear the entry, tap the same button again. Once the meal is recorded, it shrinks to one line; tap Change to adjust it.

For a precise record, tap the details button beside the meal to open the meal details and enter the amount offered and the amount left over, using the same unit for both. Grams are the most accurate. You can also add a note, such as "ate slowly" or "vomited afterward."

If your pet eats less than half of a meal, DiaPetic reminds you to follow your veterinarian's instructions before giving insulin.

Recording a shot

  1. Tap Give under Insulin, which shows the prescribed dose, for example "Give 8 units."
  2. Choose when the shot was given: Just now, At the scheduled time, or A different time… to enter the exact time.
  3. Insulin then shows a check with the dose and time, for example "8 units at 7:04 AM," so anyone checking later can see that it was given.

If another shot is already logged within about 10 hours of the time you chose, DiaPetic asks whether you are sure before saving. This check helps when two people care for the same pet. Tap Log this shot to save it, or Cancel to save nothing. The check only compares the times in your records. It never suggests a dose.

To change the time or units, add a note, or record a skipped shot (for example, when your veterinarian has advised skipping a dose because your pet did not eat), tap the details button beside the shot. You can also delete an entry made by mistake.

If you are unsure whether a shot was given, do not give another one. Check the Tracker first. If the shot is not recorded and no one remembers giving it, call your veterinarian. The emergency screen includes a guide for this situation.

Medications, supplements and treats

Medications and supplements for the morning or evening appear as buttons under Medications in that routine. Tap one to check it off. To change when it was given, press and hold it and choose Change the time. Medications on their own schedule and medications given as needed are in the Medications tile, which appears when your pet has any of these.

Supplements given with a meal appear in that meal's routine, and supplements given once a day appear in the morning routine. An unchecked supplement never keeps a routine from counting as done, so a forgotten supplement can never hide the next shot.

To record a treat from your list, tap Add a treat and enter how many were given. For anything outside the meal plan, such as table scraps or a snack your pet found, tap Something else. Extra food can raise blood sugar, so these entries are useful to your vet.

Weight

Tap the Log weight tile to record a weight. For a reliable trend, weigh your pet at about the same time of day on the same scale. If your vet gives a body condition score at a checkup (on a scale of 1 to 9, where 4 to 5 is usually ideal), you can record it here as well.

The daily check-in

Water intake, urination, stool, energy and sleep are set to Normal by default and are saved that way, so you only need to change what was different. Turn on Vomited if your pet vomited that day, and enter a Water amount if you measure it. Drinking and urinating more than usual can indicate that blood sugar is running high.

The journal

The Journal tile is for anything else worth noting: how the day went, a change in behavior, or a question for your vet.

  1. Tap Write about today, or Add another entry if the day already has one.
  2. Tap the microphone and speak. Your words are transcribed as you talk, with punctuation; tap again to stop. You can also type, or correct the text afterward.
  3. Confirm the time and tap Save.

A day can have several entries. Tap an entry to edit it, or swipe left to delete it. Journal entries appear in History, in Reports, in the PDF report and in the clinic summary on the emergency screen. In Reports, the journal is collapsed to keep the page short. Tap it to see the latest entries, or tap All entries to see every entry in the period.

Glucose and ketones

If you check your pet's glucose or ketones at home, record them from the Glucose & ketones tile. The tile shows the latest glucose reading and, once a ketone test is logged that day, its result. The Glucose, ketones and lab results chapter covers this in detail.

Walks

Record a walk from the Walks tile with the time it started and how long it lasted. Exercise can lower blood glucose, so the walk appears as a shaded band on that day's glucose curve.

Supplies

The Insulin Supplies section at the bottom of today's page shows which item will run out next, along with the insulin bottle in use, syringes and food on hand. See Insulin, syringes and food.

Correcting an earlier day

To review or correct a previous day, move back with the calendar or a swipe, or open the History tab. History groups logged days by month, with the newest month open. Tap any month to open or close it. Each day shows its shots (a filled icon for given, an X for skipped and an empty circle for not logged), the glucose range and the first journal entry. Tap a day to open it and make changes, just as you would for today.
