The Daily Tracker

The Tracker tab is where each day happens. It opens on today. If you come back after more than five minutes away, or the next day, it jumps back to today on its own.

Moving between days

  • Swipe left or right across the page, or tap the ‹ › arrows beside the date.
  • Tap the calendar button beside "Daily Tracker" to jump straight to any day, or use the quick jumps: today, one week, two weeks or one month ago.
  • Tap the date to come back to today.
  • When you change days, you stay at the same spot on the page, so you can compare the same section day to day.

At a glance and alerts

The top of the page shows today's calories against the target, the latest glucose reading, and how many meals are logged. Banners appear when something needs attention:

  • Eating less than usual when recent meals were small.
  • Low reading when a glucose reading is below your low number, with buttons to call your vet or emergency clinic.
  • An upcoming appointment the day before and the day of a visit, with what to bring.

Logging a meal

Each morning and evening card starts with the meal.

  • Tap All, Most, Some or None for how much was eaten. Tap the same button again to clear it.
  • For exact amounts, tap the meal row to open the meal details. Enter the amount offered and the amount left over in the same unit (grams are the most accurate), and add a note such as "picky" or "vomited".
  • If less than half the meal was eaten, DiaPetic reminds you to check your veterinarian's instructions before giving insulin.

Logging a shot

  1. Tap Give on the Insulin row. It shows the prescribed units, for example "Give 8 u".
  2. Choose when it was given: Just now, At the scheduled time, or A different time… to pick the exact time.
  3. The row then shows, for example, "8 units given at 7:04 AM", so anyone checking later knows it's done.

Tap the details button beside the shot to change the time or units, add a note, mark the shot as skipped (for example when your pet didn't eat and your vet said to skip), or delete an entry made by mistake.

Not sure whether a shot was given? Don't give another. Check the Tracker first. If it isn't logged and no one remembers giving it, call your veterinarian. The emergency screen has a guide for this.

Medications, supplements and treats

  • Morning and evening medications appear in the matching card. Tap to check one off; tap its time to change when it was given.
  • As-needed medications have their own section. Tap Give each time one is given.
  • Supplements show as a simple daily checkbox.
  • Meals and treats: tap Add a treat to log a treat from your list (with how many), or Something else for food outside the plan, like table scraps or a stolen snack. Extra food can raise blood sugar, so it helps your vet to know.

Weight

Tap the Weight section to log a weight for the day. Weigh at about the same time of day on the same scale for the most useful trend. At checkups, your vet may give a body condition score from 1 to 9 (4 to 5 is usually ideal); you can record that here too.

The daily check-in

Water, urine, stool, energy and sleep all start at Normal and are saved that way, so you only change what was different. Turn on Vomited if it happened. If you measure water, enter the amount under Water amount. Drinking and urinating more than usual can be a sign that blood sugar is running high.

The journal

Under the check-in, the Journal is for anything else worth remembering: how the day went, something new, a question for your vet.

  1. Tap Write about today (or Add another entry).
  2. Tap the big microphone and just talk; your words appear as text, with punctuation. Tap again to stop. Or type, or fix words afterward.
  3. Check the time and tap Save.

A day can have several entries. Tap one to edit it; swipe left to delete it. Journal entries appear in History, in Reports, in the PDF report, and in the clinic summary on the emergency screen.

Glucose readings

If you check glucose at home, add readings in the Blood glucose section. See Glucose and lab results. If you don't, the section stays small and out of the way.

Supplies

At the bottom of today's page, Supplies shows what runs out next, with the insulin bottle, syringes and food on hand. See Insulin, syringes and food.

Fixing an earlier day (History)

Go back with the calendar or a swipe, or open the History tab. It lists every logged day with the shots (a filled icon for given, an X for skipped, an empty circle for not logged), the glucose range, and the first journal entry. Tap a day to open it and add or fix anything, just like today.
