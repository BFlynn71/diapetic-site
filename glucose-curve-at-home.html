How to track a dog or cat's glucose curve at home

By the developer of DiaPetic, who cares for Obi, a diabetic Sheepadoodle · Updated November 18, 2026

A glucose curve is a set of blood sugar readings taken across one day. Your vet uses it to see how your pet's insulin is working: when blood sugar is lowest, how long the insulin lasts, and whether readings stay in the range your vet wants.

Many vets now ask owners to do curves at home. A pet at home is calmer and eats as usual, which can give a truer picture than a day at the clinic. This matters most for cats, whose blood sugar can rise from the stress of a clinic visit.

This guide is about keeping good records. Your vet decides the schedule, the target range and any change to insulin.

Before the curve day

  • Ask your vet when to start, how often to test and which reading means you should call. Veterinary guidelines describe a reading before the meal and shot, then about every two hours for 10 to 12 hours, with checks more often when readings are low.
  • Use the meter your vet recommends. Veterinary guidelines favor meters calibrated for dogs and cats, because meters made for people can show different numbers for the same drop of blood. Tell your vet which one you use.
  • Plan an ordinary day, with the usual food, meal times and walks.
  • Set out test strips, lancets, cotton balls and a small reward for afterward.

What to write down

A number on its own does not tell your vet much. Record what happened around it:

  • The time of each reading and the result
  • The time of the morning shot and the amount given, as prescribed
  • The time of each meal and how much was eaten
  • Walks, play, treats and anything unusual, such as vomiting, shaking or tiredness
  • Readings that had to be repeated, and why

Write each reading down when you take it. Times written in later are easy to get wrong.

When to stop and call

If a reading is below the low number your vet gave you, or your pet seems weak, wobbly, shaky or confused, or has a seizure, call your veterinarian or an emergency clinic now. DiaPetic has an emergency button on every screen with a low blood sugar guide. See When something seems wrong.
